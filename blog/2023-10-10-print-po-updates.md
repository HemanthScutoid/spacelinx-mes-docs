We’re excited to announce an update to the **Purchase Order (PO) printing feature** in Spacelinx MES!

Previously, when printing a PO, you had to print **all columns** by default. Now, you have the **flexibility to choose exactly what information you want to include** in your printed documents.

### What’s new?

When you click **Print PO**, a new **Print Options dialog** will appear. Here’s what you can do:

- **Select which fields to include:** Part Number, HSN, Manufacturing Part Number, Tax Group, Tax Value, and Tax Amount.
- **Easier readability:** Only include the information you need, making your printed PO cleaner and more focused.
- **Quick and simple:** Just tick the checkboxes and click **Print** — no extra steps required.

### Why this is useful

- Print **custom POs** for different departments or purposes.
- Reduce paper usage by printing only relevant information.
- Make it easier for your team to review and approve POs.

### How to use it

1. Navigate to the **Purchase Orders** section.
2. Click **Print PO** on the PO you want to print.
3. Tick the checkboxes for the fields you want to include.
4. Click **Print**. That’s it — your customized PO will be ready to use.

We hope this update makes handling Purchase Orders even easier for your daily workflow!
