SpaceLinx MES (Manufacturing Execution System) is an advanced digital platform designed to streamline aerospace and high-precision manufacturing workflows.  
It bridges the gap between planning and production, ensuring **real-time visibility**, **traceability**, and **process control** across your entire manufacturing lifecycle.

---

## 🚀 What is SpaceLinx MES?

SpaceLinx MES is part of the SpaceLinx Digital Suite — a modular, scalable ecosystem for manufacturing and engineering excellence.  
It integrates multiple domains such as **PLM (Product Lifecycle Management)**, **Procurement**, **Inventory**, and **Production** — enabling teams to operate in a unified environment.

---

## 🧠 Core Capabilities

- **PLM Integration:** Centralized product and parts data management with real-time BOM synchronization.
- **Production Control:** Plan, execute, and track manufacturing processes across multiple work centers.
- **Procurement Automation:** Streamlined purchase orders and supplier collaboration with complete traceability.
- **Inventory Management:** Live stock tracking with location-wise bin management and material movement history.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** KPIs and performance tracking with data-driven insights for operational improvement.
