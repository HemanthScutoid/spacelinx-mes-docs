# 🧾 Edit Goods Inventory

The **Edit Goods Inventory** page allows you to view and manage details of a specific goods item that already exists in your inventory.  
It helps you track stock levels, view transaction history, and update item details — all in one place.

---

## 🪧 What You Can Do

The page is divided into three main tabs to help you easily navigate and manage your goods inventory.

---

### 🧱 1. Inventory Tab

- Displays the **current stock information** of the selected goods item.
- Shows how much of the item is currently available.
- You can use this section to get an overview of stock levels at different locations.

---

### 📊 2. Transactions Tab

- Shows a **history of all inventory transactions** related to this goods item.
- Each record includes:
  - **Date** of the transaction
  - **Type** (for example: Received, Issued, or Adjusted)
  - **Created By** (the user who performed the action)
  - **Previous Quantity**, **Current Quantity**, and **Transacted Quantity**
  - **From Location** and **To Location** details
  - **Reference** to related purchase orders or processes
- You can click on a **reference number** to open the related purchase order or record in a new tab.

---

### 📝 3. Details Tab

- Displays the **core details** of the goods item, such as:
  - **Name**
  - **Description**

#### ✏️ Editing Details

- To edit, click the **Edit (pencil)** icon at the top-right corner.
- The fields will become editable.
- You can:
  - Change the name or description.
  - Click **Update** to save your changes.
  - Click **Reset** to revert to the original values.
  - Click **Cancel** to close without saving.

> ⚠️ Note: Only users with the appropriate permissions can edit goods details.  
> If you don’t have edit access, you’ll see a warning message.

---

## ⚙️ What Happens When You Update

- The system validates that the **Name** field is filled in.
- Once updated, a success message appears confirming your changes.
- The goods item information is refreshed to show the latest details.

---

## 💡 Notes

- Use the **Transactions tab** to monitor any changes in inventory levels over time.
- Keep item names clear and descriptive for easier searching later.
- If you only need to review information, you can stay in **read-only mode** — no accidental edits will occur.
