# 🧾 Create New Goods Inventory

The **Create Goods Inventory** page allows you to add a new goods item to your inventory list.

---

## 🪧 What You Can Do

- Enter the **Name** of the goods item you want to add.  
  _(This is a required field — you won’t be able to save without it.)_

- Optionally, you can add a **Description** to provide more details about the goods.

- After filling in the details:
  - Click **Create** to save the new item.
  - Click **Cancel** if you don’t want to proceed.

---

## ⚙️ What Happens After You Click “Create”

- The system checks that all required fields are filled in.
- Once submitted, the goods item is added to the inventory list.
- A **success message** appears to confirm the creation.
- The inventory list automatically refreshes to display the new entry.

---

## 💡 Notes

- Provide a clear and unique **Name** for each goods item to avoid confusion.
- The **Description** helps others understand what the goods item is or how it’s used — but it’s optional.
